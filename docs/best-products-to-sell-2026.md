# Best Products To Sell In 2026

WYX should sell products that make a golfer think, "I could use that this weekend." The store should not look like a generic supplier catalog. The best assortment is a tight mix of personality gifts, trip/cart gear, bag organization, apparel, and a small number of practice/tech products.

## Product Thesis

Prioritize:

- Under-$60 accessories that can be bought without fitting help.
- Products with real supplier imagery and clean variant/color matching.
- Items that work for golf trips, dads, bachelor parties, scrambles, and weekend players.
- Repeatable consumables: gloves, balls, tees, towels, grips, and markers.
- A controlled tech/training expansion with products that solve a clear problem.

Avoid:

- Fake WYX-branded mockups until actual WYX merchandise exists.
- Products where the first image does not match the selected variant.
- Expensive simulator components, hitting screens, replacement cases, and other technical clutter.
- No-inventory products, placeholder images, vague customization products, and non-golf adjacent gadgets.

## Import Rules

Before activating a product:

1. Supplier path is confirmed: Shopify Collective, wholesale, POD, owned stock, or documented dropship terms.
2. Featured image matches the default/selected variant.
3. Every color variant has either a matching image or the product copy clearly explains the image/color relationship.
4. Product is in stock and has at least one purchasable variant.
5. Target retail is under $75 unless it is a deliberate premium category page product.
6. Product page has a "Why it belongs in your bag" section.
7. Tags include intent: `gift`, `trip-gear`, `bag-upgrade`, `training-aid`, `dad-gift`, or `scramble-prize`.
8. Product is included in at least one kit, collection, or recommendation path.

## Channel Strategy

Use products this way:

- Homepage: 3-6 strongest products only, led by glove, balls, hat, caddie, towel, marker pack.
- Gift pages: under-$60, personality, low sizing risk.
- Trip pages: balls, towels, markers, caddies, coolers, pouches, shoe bags.
- Bag upgrades: headcovers, caddies, towels, brushes, grips.
- Training: putting mirror, gate trainer, tempo trainer, rangefinder.
- Apparel: hats first, then polos/tees once images and sizing charts are clean.
