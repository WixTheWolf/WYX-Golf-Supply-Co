## Current Merchandising Decision

- Homepage and offer pages should feature only core merch: towels, markers, balls, caddies, grips, gloves, and under-$75 headcovers with real photos and plausible supplier path.
- Premium bags are removed from the core funnel and reserved for `/premium-golf-bags`.
- SYB Phone Pouch is hidden from core storefront grids because it does not support the golf gifts/trip gear/bag upgrades positioning.
- All placeholder-image drafts remain hidden.
