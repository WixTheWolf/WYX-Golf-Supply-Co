- URL: https://jandmgolf.com/
- Categories: 6,000+ golf accessories — markers, divot tools, towels, tees, brushes, bag tags. Directly covers priorities 2–8.
- Terms: family-owned US wholesale distributor since 1987; known for in-stock status and same-day shipping. Wholesale accounts for retailers; dropship support reported by third-party directories — **confirm dropship terms on outreach**.
- Shopify integration: no public app; likely CSV/manual or EDI. Workable at WYX volume.
- MOQ: wholesale account likely requires modest opening order — confirm.
- Margin potential: strong (true wholesale, not aggregator markup).
- Shipping: US domestic, fast.
- Best for WYX: marker/divot sets, towels, tee products, brushes — kit components at better margin.
- Risk: LOW. Established US distributor.
- Next step: wholesale account application + ask explicitly about dropship/blind-ship.
