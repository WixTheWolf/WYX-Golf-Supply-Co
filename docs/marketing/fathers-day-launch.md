## Pre-launch checklist (owner, ~15 min — these gate real revenue)
1. **Place one real test order** end-to-end and confirm the payout reaches your bank. This is the only thing that can't be verified from outside.
2. Confirm the Meta Pixel is firing Purchase events (Events Manager → Test Events).
3. Set a daily budget you're comfortable losing while creatives find their footing.
4. Launch Meta Ad Sets A–C + the Google RSA. Give it 3–4 days before judging.
